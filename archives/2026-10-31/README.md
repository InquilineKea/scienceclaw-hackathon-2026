# Original and corrected archives

Use **fracture-atlas-frozen-20261031.zip** for the finished atlas with final results and matching regenerated diagrams/animations. Its manifest covers source, numerical data, logs and figures. Historical rendering input is clearly labeled inside it.

`fracture-atlas-complete.zip` and `fracture-atlas-data-source.zip` are the original received archives, preserved unchanged. The former contains graphics made from an older rendering input. Do not substitute these for the corrected frozen archive when interpreting final pulse results.

`public-scienceclaw-collagen-results-complete.zip` is the previously published molecular package. Additional available raw attempt files and prior GROMACS trajectory files are unpacked under collagen-molecular-pilot elsewhere in this repository.

`collagen-pilot-results.zip` is the previously downloaded and verified GROMACS baseline, not a newly executed MD study. `collagen-mechanics-explorer-with-brev-evidence.zip` is the prior explorer with an evidence panel; external dependencies and launch extensions were not executed as part of this archive update.
