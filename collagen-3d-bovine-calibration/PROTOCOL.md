# Bovine bulk calibration and conditional 3D fracture experiment

Frozen before new mechanics runs, 2026-10-31.

Use the archived periodic Voronoi geometries (seeds 29–32), with their original segment stretching and junction cosine-angle energies. Geometry is synthetic, not reconstructed bovine collagen. Baseline coordination is 3.8; nested degree-preserving pruning is deterministic and preserves connectedness and minimum degree two. Periodic simple shear is applied in xy and xz. Translation is removed by fixing node zero; no background mechanical stabilizer is added.

Calibration: reuse the independently replayed bovine same-study Kelvin–Voigt fit, selected for the best mean whole-file held-out complex NRMSE among the three existing candidates. It has G′=9.52223163(c/1.5)^2.2 Pa and G″=3.89114025(c/1.5)^1.1 f Hz Pa. Primary domain: 37°C, 1% oscillatory strain, 0.8–2.3 mg/mL, 0.1–1 Hz. The high-frequency challenge remains excluded. Fit uncertainty is substantial (mean held-out complex NRMSE 0.498). This is not independent validation. Baseline network energy is multiplied by target G′ divided by each seed's mean xy/xz small-strain modulus. The same factor remains frozen for all modifications of that seed. A parallel homogenized dashpot matches G″; it is omitted from quasistatic fracture. Neither microscopic viscosity nor fiber modulus, physical volume, permeability, age, or rupture is identified by this calibration.

Crosslink surrogate: select 15% or 30% of intact junctions (same counts for both patterns), and multiply their angular penalty by 10. Distributed sites use seeded shuffling; clustered sites are nearest to a seeded center by periodic distance. This changes angular locking, not the number of added physical chemical bonds. Publish spatial spread and orientation balance as diagnostics; do not claim placement-only causality if those differ. The baseline segment stiffness and graph are identical within each seed.

Fracture is a separately assumed irreversible segment extension threshold: 20%, with a 10% sensitivity representing additional fragility. Apply the same threshold to every segment, including controls. These are hypotheses, not calibrated aging chemistry. Delete all failed segments and their associated angular constraints; re-equilibrate at fixed shear until no additional failures. Record explicit elastic energy lost on deletion and subsequent relaxation, stress, damage fraction, and work density. Work to peak is a specimen response, not a notched fracture toughness Gc. No phase change is identified.

Primary sweep: four seeds × two shear planes × five locking configurations (baseline; two densities × two patterns) × two rupture thresholds, at 100 increments up to shear 0.5. Repeat at 200 increments for step sensitivity. Report all failed/capped cases. No inference of complete separation from reaching the cap. Convergence requires max free-node gradient ≤1e-7 and finite state; discarded or unresolved states must not contribute to mechanical summary claims. Reference checks include rest energy, finite-difference gradient/stress, and comparison with archived full-connectivity 3D small-strain energies. A numerical check is not material validation.

Publish measured-versus-fit rheology, small-strain stiffness ratios, peak stress and work-to-peak ratios, paired seed/angle differences, convergence and step sensitivity, and an interactive 3D force/damage animation. Aging interpretation is conditional throughout; no beaver/human transfer.

The new solver is original dependency-free JavaScript, running without executing imported packages or repository scripts. No new cloud cost is necessary if local commissioning succeeds. Preserve source, inputs, raw trajectories, exclusions, and hashes.

## Pre-production amendment after adversarial audit

The original site-only commissioning series is preserved in `stiffness-site-only.json`, `models-before-degree-control.json`, `solver-before-degree-control.mjs`, and the interrupted nonlinear runs. It is not pooled with production. Distributed selection now matches the clustered set's exact count of selected degree-2/3/4 junctions within each seed/density. Therefore selected-site counts, active angular-term counts, and the total added angular coefficient are identical, with the same 10× local locking multiplier. Spatial spread and orientation remain diagnostics. Production uses the vectorized implementation of exactly this amended model, with independent JavaScript checks. Capped curves are explicitly censored; work through the strain cap is not work to a resolved peak. A 2% work/energy gate and 5% step sensitivity gate qualify work/peak comparisons. Selected true-Hessian checks screen potential saddle equilibria; report coverage rather than assuming a small gradient proves stability. Calibration model-selection CV is not untouched validation. Synthetic perseed bulk matching is a controlled intervention baseline, not Campaign1 measured-architecture validation; the latter needs a shared material law fit on training specimens with specimen/batch holdouts.
