# Independent output comparison and qualification plan

Frozen before reviewing new CPU outputs. This is an audit gate plan, not permission to execute third-party code locally. The JavaScript solver is original task-authored code; the Python numerical runtime remains remote-only.

1. **Transport and source identity.** Verify the downloaded ZIP SHA256 against the final receipt, reject unsafe ZIP paths, preserve the receipt and partial/error records, and confirm the returned source and models match `frozen-input-readback.json`. A successful provider status alone is insufficient. Check expected 160 final trajectory identities; partial files are not additional runs.

2. **Remote preflight.** Require all four `remote-checks.json` records and their recorded package/runtime versions. Recompute the archived energy comparison from the saved reference values where available. Its prescribed gate is relative energy difference <0.001, derivative errors <1e-7, and relaxed free gradient <=1e-9. Missing/failed preflight blocks use of production outputs. This compares the synthetic constitutive equations, not bovine observations.

3. **Exact low-strain comparison to JavaScript.** Deduplicate 200-step initial snapshots by seed, plane, pattern and density (40 distinct configurations; rupture thresholds are repeated controls at the initial strain). Use each snapshot's exact gamma, frozen model and active mask. Independently evaluate its energy, stress and nodal gradient using the standard-library equation audit. Then, where additional original-code execution is useful, relax the same frozen model with the authored JavaScript solver at the same gamma and a free-gradient target <=1e-8. Compare energy and stress, not node coordinates, because gauge, disconnected modes or alternate weak directions can differ. Predeclared provisional comparison gates: energy relative difference <=0.001 and stress relative difference <=0.005, with denominator floors declared. Do not compare the remote gamma=.0025 stress directly to the gamma=.001 calibration slope and label the difference a solver error.

4. **Equilibrium and completeness.** Require every included state to be accepted, finite and within free-gradient tolerance <=1e-7. Preserve unresolved curves as censored diagnostics. Complete-trajectory claims require gamma=0.5 and `trajectoryComplete=true`. A complete curve may still be right-censored for peak/failure. A resolved sampled peak requires the production operational rule; capped maxima are not fracture peaks.

5. **Energy bookkeeping.** Independently recompute terminal and selected-state `W - [U-U0 + removal + damage-relaxation]` from saved fields. Confirm removal and fixed-strain relaxation are nonnegative within numerical precision. For whole-trajectory work claims require terminal relative residual <=0.02 using max(|W|,1e-12). Also report the maximum residual at loaded states with |W| >= max(1e-12,0.05*terminal |W|), avoiding inflated percentages at undeformed/near-zero states. A large residual may indicate unresolved branch-snap dissipation or insufficient loading increments; do not reclassify it as validated fracture energy.

6. **Loading-step sensitivity.** Pair 100/200-step runs by seed, plane, pattern, density and rupture threshold. Compare only complete, equilibrium-qualified counterparts. At common imposed strains, compare stresses and accumulated work; the two first-positive strains differ, so their raw initial secants are not an exact matched-strain check. Provisional endpoint/observed-peak gates: work-through-cap and peak-stress relative changes <=5%, and peak-strain difference <=0.01 shear. Work-to-peak comparisons require resolved peaks in both curves. Damage-fraction differences >0.02 absolute are flagged as event-sequence sensitivity, with exact values retained. If a placement conclusion changes sign with step resolution, report it as unresolved rather than averaging the resolutions.

7. **True-curvature coverage.** Summarize all selected-state Hessian classifications and expected versus actual coverage: three snapshots per complete 200-step trajectory, while some initial/peak/terminal states may coincide. Confirm saved active masks match the stage being audited. Exclude confirmed negative-curvature checkpoints from qualified peak/terminal claims. Treat finite-difference accuracy failures, non-equilibrated states and unresolved negative modes as unqualified. Report marginal states separately; no resolved negative mode is not proof of rigidity. No screen exists at the many intermediate states unless explicitly computed.

8. **Paired inference.** Keep each seed/plane/pattern comparison paired with its baseline and opposite placement at identical density and threshold. The independent replicate count is four seeds; two planes and two rupture thresholds are repeated conditions, not independent specimens. Report all eight seed-angle signs plus per-seed means/ranges, qualified-pair counts and exclusions. A pattern advantage unsupported across seeds, angles, step sizes or curvature/energy gates is conditional. Do not replace missing/unqualified pairs with zero effects.

9. **Physical validation boundary.** Keep bovine primary-band G′/G″ fit/holdout errors separate from numerical comparisons. Synthetic 3D geometry, angular-lock density, rupture thresholds and energy-density scaling are assumptions. No result in this sweep independently validates measured fiber architecture, nonlinear rheology, local strain, AGE chemistry, species differences, complete separation or Gc. Those need the measured-architecture Campaign 1 evidence and separately withheld targets.
