It used full AIMNet2 model members0/3 separately, axial/rotation scans, force-derived scalar fits, numerical/geometry gates, and conditional 3D network transfer. ## Chemistry/potential validation still missing

Passing bond-length, stereocenter, force-residual and grip tests verifies selected geometry and numerical properties. It does not independently validate the dityrosine force-field energy surface. Two members of the same AIMNet family are model-ensemble alternatives, not independent reference methods. A classical force field and an ML potential can disagree because both are approximations, their environments differ, or their reference structures differ; agreement alone does not validate chemistry.

The next independent reference should target a small, chemically unambiguous observable on the **same charge/protonation/connectivity/stereochemistry and matched coordinate definition**: low-deformation generalized loads and the2×2 local stretch/rotation Hessian of a defined capped dityrosine analogue plus its matched unlinked control, over several prespecified conformers. Use a separately reviewed reference electronic-structure method (and an explicit solvent/continuum comparison if the observable is hydration-sensitive) for a tractable fragment. Preserve reference energies, forces, optimizer convergence and method settings. The study should compare curves and Hessian components in common units, not just a single equilibrium bond length or an unconstrained conformer ranking. This is a proposal, not an executed quantum benchmark or authorization for new molecular spending.

For classical wet scans, resolve the global force/energy finite-difference failure first or retain wet coefficients as unidentified. Suggested diagnostic comparison: identical frozen peptide coordinates, exact same solvent box and constraints, then vary only the nonbonded smoothing/cutoff/grid choice and finite-difference epsilon. A changed Hamiltonian is a new model/version, not a numerical rescue under the old chemistry. Compare energy and physical generalized force under exactly the same constraint manifold. Do not infer “solvent removes coupling” from failed wet curvature identification.

## Justified molecule-to-fiber averaging

The current network transfer maps normalized axial/angular changes and rho onto model terms. This is an explicit constitutive scenario, not an identified fiber law. A peptide local tangent law must not be equated directly with the axial rigidity or bending stiffness of a full fiber segment.

Define the coarse coordinate map and energy equality first. For local molecular coordinates q=(strain,rotation), write the quadratic energy as E0+f0·q+½qᵀHq. If network coordinates z map locally through q=Tz, the corresponding contribution is TᵀHT and the prestress term is Tᵀf0. T must include the assumed number/orientation of load-bearing molecules, series/parallel load sharing, molecular/segment reference lengths, and the molecular-rotation to junction-cosine relation. If this mapping is unavailable, retain a range of stated mappings and label the resulting network effects conditional. A positive-definite normalized rho term proves energy admissibility of the chosen network extension; it does not identify T.

The angular coordinate deserves particular care. A molecular rotation in radians maps to a junction cosine change through a rest-angle-dependent derivative; the sign follows the explicitly stated closing/opening convention. Near-collinear angles make a naive inverse sine mapping ill-conditioned. Report the rest-angle domain, excluded geometries and nonlinear window. Do not silently set this Jacobian to one, clip rho, repair eigenvalues, force a stiffness sign or average failed fits into a passing law. Keep finite molecular prestress separate from a network reference state that has zero stress by construction.

Averaging also depends on how elements carry load. Parallel contributions under common strain add stiffness matrices; series elements require compliance-based reduction under common load. A median of linked/control ratios over correlated frames is a robust scenario summary, but it is not either physical homogenization. Average accepted energies/loads or appropriate matrices/compliances according to a justified assembly, propagate reference-length and orientation uncertainty, and state the averaging measure. Averaging a numerator and denominator separately differs from averaging ratios; near-zero or unidentified denominators must remain excluded/unidentified with counts.

Keep model family, environment and replica separate. Pooling vacuum AIMNet, classical wet and classical dry curvatures into one “best stiffness” discards model-form uncertainty. For every coefficient, report how many linked/control pairs passed, why others failed and whether selection favors particular environments/conformers. A numerical fit standard error estimates local fit precision, not biological variability.

## Independent units and network tests

Three short prior replicas are the highest sampling level presently available; their snapshots and deformation points are repeated measurements. They are not known equilibrium samples. Model members0/3 and water molecules are not independent biological replicates. Network seeds are independent synthetic realizations; donor/narrow/broad geometries, exposure fractions, signaling settings and repeated load steps are correlated within seed. Use paired effects within each replica/potential/environment and network seed, then show their spread and sign counts. A large count of network states does not create hundreds of independent molecular or tissue specimens.

The requested +/-mixed-term comparison should retain the same diagonal coefficients, reference state, geometry, loading, selected elements and placements. Include coupled-unlinked as well as coupled-linked controls: simply comparing rho=0 to linked rho can confuse baseline coupling with the chemical difference. Preserve the existing diagonal-only, coupled-unlinked and coupled-linked arms. Also compare both raw forces and matched-initial-stiffness results; scalar matching is an isolation control that changes the intervention, not the absolute chemical prediction. Mean-force normalization can reveal heterogeneity beyond mean load but must be shown beside unnormalized load transmission.

Screen every network state's local strain/angle excursion against the molecular fit window. Transfer of a ±1%/±5° tangent law into much larger segment/junction excursions is extrapolation even if the network solver converges. Report the fraction of elements and energy outside the valid molecular window, and do not call those arms molecularly calibrated. Full fiber torsion, sliding, failure or kinetic memory remain separate constitutive requirements.

## Defensible next observable and stopping gate

A tractable primary molecular observable is the matched linked–unlinked change in the local symmetric stretch/rotation Hessian (including rho) under one explicitly specified environment and reference conformation ensemble. First require numerical force/energy consistency, geometry admissibility and independent-reference agreement at a tolerance set from reference accuracy and within-ensemble spread. Then propagate the accepted matrix and mapping uncertainty into a prespecified network observable such as initial contraction/shear stiffness and interface-force concentration, comparing identical networks with versus without the mixed term.

Do not select a molecular mapping or response parameter because it yields a desirable YAP or network sign. If the wet Hessian remains unidentified, the conclusion is that the current evidence cannot quantify wet coupled transfer; publish the failure and maintain vacuum/sensitivity arms as separate hypotheses. No additional molecular run should begin merely because a GPU is available. Before new compute, define which missing observable, reference calculation or uncertainty the run will resolve and check the active registry so the existing running campaign is reused.
