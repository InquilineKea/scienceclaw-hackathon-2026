# Three collagen campaigns: evidence, current status and validation gates

Evidence handoff, 31 October 2026. This document accompanies the bovine-calibrated synthetic comparator. Bulk rheology calibration and numerical solver checks do not establish that a model reproduces measured 3D collagen architecture, aging chemistry, fracture or organism-specific robustness. **Physical validation against measured 3D architecture has not been achieved.** No data requests were sent and no new compute was run to prepare this handoff.

## Campaign 1 — Measured 3D collagen architecture: highest priority

The available [bovine hydrogel rheology archive](https://zenodo.org/records/17413651) permits source-specific calibration of small-amplitude G′ and G″. Its retrieved frequency-file inventory contains ten bovine sweeps, 210 observations, across 0.8, 1.5 and 2.3 mg/mL. These constrain a bulk response within the measured protocol; they do not uniquely identify fiber bending, junction chemistry or network geometry.

[Núñez-Ortega et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mtbio.2025.101962) provides the closest bovine nanoarchitecture reference: three 111.8 µm³ reconstructed volumes, about 2,100 segments per volume, overall segment arclength 346.55 ± 338.75 nm and endpoint distance 302.70 ± 283.37 nm (mean ± SD). Published orientation and tortuosity moments are also available. **Raw measured 3D architecture is available on request according to the paper; it has not been recovered here.** Coordinates, segment polylines, adjacency, degree distributions, full orientation/size distributions and spatial correlations remain missing. Published moments can constrain an explicitly synthetic ensemble, but cannot establish preservation of measured distributions or topology. Fixation/dehydration adds uncertainty when translating image dimensions into hydrated mechanics.

The decisive gate is specimen-linked imaging plus mechanical observations, with complete independent specimens or batches withheld before fitting. Freeze parameters, then predict G′, G″, nonlinear stiffening and local strain on those reserved specimens. Frequency points, fibers and overlapping image volumes are not independent biological replicates. The 2024 rheology and 2025 architecture studies must not be assumed specimen-matched.

The [NIST DVC Challenge 2.0](https://data.nist.gov/od/id/mds2-4129) offers an external measured-deformation route: experimental collagen images around a contracting inclusion, separately from synthetic ground-truth images. Provenance, boundaries and displacement uncertainty must be checked before use; this is not matched bovine rheology. [PDB 1CAG](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1CAG) is a short collagen-like molecular triple helix, not a measured fiber network or material calibration.

## Campaign 2 — One chemically validated crosslink-to-network bridge

The current owning molecular report records the solvent-coupled dityrosine study as **done / unvalidated**: 78/78 wet deformation states and 6/6 references completed, but **0/3 paired wet laws passed transfer**. All six individual wet mixed profiles fail the stated energy/force-Hessian consistency and fit-window stability gates. Completed calculations therefore do not supply an admissible hydrated stretch–rotation law.

The audited chemical graph is a defined neutral-phenol, carbon–carbon dityrosine model in an intramolecular telopeptide loop. It is not a measured intermolecular fibril junction. Selected dry scenarios remain conditional; they do not validate wet behavior. The next gates are an independent electronic-structure benchmark of the relevant chemistry, resolution of wet energy/force inconsistency, an environment-matched ensemble observable, and an energy-consistent map through molecular orientation and series/parallel load sharing to fiber/network properties. [CGenFF parameter guidance](https://docs.silcsbio.com/2025/cgenff/usage.html) and [OpenMM force definitions](https://docs.openmm.org/latest/userguide/theory/02_standard_forces.html) clarify parameter and numerical checks; neither validates this particular crosslink law. No wet coefficient should be transferred into the comparator as a validated aging effect.

## Campaign 3 — Comparable type-I sources before species claims

The narrowest available comparison is bovine dermis versus rat tail type-I gels in the [2024 quantitative hydrogel atlas](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actbio.2024.07.002): non-pepsinized acidified collagen, matched concentrations and medium, pH 7.4, 37°C assembly and small-amplitude rheology. Species remains confounded with tissue and supplier. The downloadable [institutional figure archive](https://doi.org/10.12795/11441/188036) contains ten TIFF figures, including BOV/RAT/MIX frequency traces and structural summaries; it contains no raw replicate tables or 3D graphs.

A raw bovine bulk fit can run now. A BOV/RAT figure-digitized bulk comparison is possible after checking axes, trace aggregation, error-bar meaning and pixel uncertainty. Do not manufacture replicates from plotted lines. Matched raw RAT/MIX histories, specimen identifiers and independent held-out batches are still needed for stronger validation.

[Burla et al. (2020)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7165426/) supplies protocol-specific nonlinear, plasticity and shear-fracture challenges, with raw data on request. Its reported connectivity is inferred through rheology/model calibration, not an independent measured 3D degree distribution; shear failure peaks are not notched fracture toughness. Future source comparisons must separate fixed-mass, fixed-volume, fixed-connectivity and fixed-crosslink controls. The original beaver-versus-human robustness hypothesis remains open; no robustness ranking follows from these datasets.

**Actionable boundary:** Run transparent bulk-calibrated synthetic comparisons and carefully labeled figure-derived source comparisons now. Progress to measured-architecture prediction and chemical/species attribution only when their respective data, numerical consistency and independent validation gates are satisfied.
