# Constrained analytic HVP and Ritz screen review

The analytic HVP in hvp-audit.cpp matches the exact material plus geometric derivative of the condensed incremental fiber potential. It preserves the compression geometric contribution q.t/L. Its production Krylov vectors have zero components on fixed nodes, and output fixed components are zeroed; dot products exclude those coordinates. Thus this invocation computes the free/free restriction rather than a boundary-relaxed Schur complement, consistent with prescribed displacements.

The three-term Lanczos recurrence, double reorthogonalization, tridiagonal assembly and Jacobi symmetric rotations are algebraically correct. At floating-point precision the discarded reorthogonalization coefficients can make the stored tridiagonal differ slightly from the exact dense projected Hessian; this is a bounded screening algorithm, not a certified eigensolver. The stated single deterministic seed and 32-step budget are important limitations. A resolved negative Rayleigh/Ritz value detects an unstable subspace, subject to numerical accuracy; absence of a detected negative mode does not establish a local minimum, mesh convergence, stability along the path or absence of other negative modes. The relative negative threshold and absolute Jacobi convergence floor should remain reported. There is no reported Ritz-vector residual, so do not claim a fully converged minimum eigenvalue.

Use the incoming penultimate plastic state with the final displacement, not the final committed state: the latter may choose the inactive branch at the yield surface and change the tangent from condensed to elastic. Current production solver explicitly saves .previous-state.bin immediately before the final solve; that resolves the earlier checkpoint concern. All arguments must refer to the same material, angular resolution and assumed path. At exact yield transitions the potential is not twice differentiable; central differences may mix active/inactive one-sided tangents.

Per parent request, the only production-adjacent edit was to hvp-audit.cpp finite-difference diagnostics. Both perturbed objective evaluations must now have finite energy and free gradients, minJ>0.05, and a finite positive analytic norm. Invalid perturbations emit JSON null and an explicit validity array; minimum determinants are included. Numeric JSON output preserves 17-digit precision. No production solver source was edited.

All passed (extrema -0.125 and 32 in the final case).

These controlled states were not equilibrated experimental fields and are not production stability results.

This resolves the earlier zero-history coverage gap for that fixture; it does not establish all possible path or yield-switch behavior.
