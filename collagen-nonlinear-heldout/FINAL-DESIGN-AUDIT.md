Input files are read from the original measured-validation directory, so retain mask/input hashes at production and analysis: source identity does not protect against later input-file changes. Secondary displacement NRMSE remains observed-field normalized after boundary translation; it is not boundary-amplitude normalized and must retain the earlier disclosed interpretation.

The new 5×5×5 strain diagnostic fits an affine displacement map independently in each complete local stencil and computes its symmetric gradient. It admits a center only when all 125 stencil nodes belong to the same fixed eligible score mask. Thus no supplied-boundary node enters a scored stencil, including through its immediate neighborhood. Centers/stencils overlap and remain correlated. This is an equal-center local affine-gradient diagnostic, not the same weighting or derivative operator as volume-weighted raw tetrahedral P1 strain. Physical support depends on actual coordinate spacing; describe its physical extent rather than claiming five voxels. It is neither measured fiber bending nor an independent observation. Verify its unchanged harmonic value is evaluated with this exact stencil rule and frozen inputs.

## Selection and leakage safeguards

The corrected factorial has 12 arms: four buckling states, three yield states, H=0.05 and common tensile priors. Compression-absent/slip-absent is the matched constitutive control; prior ν=0 elasticity and harmonic remain separate baselines. Selecting buckling-only, sliding-only and combined representatives on Pos007, with combined primary, is consistent with the frozen plan. The families have unequal search sizes; a best-family difference alone does not identify a mechanism. Matched ablations should retain the same other coefficients.

Pos008 was already inspected in the previous study. New selection on Pos007 cannot make it a fresh independent holdout. Call the final result an adaptively reused benchmark evaluation, including a practical success criterion fixed before new nonlinear predictions. Do not tune candidates, masks, path, runtime or solvers from new Pos008 errors.

**Selection implementation guard:** analyze.mjs scans every `runs/*.config.json` without an explicit specimen filter or assertion that exactly 12 prespecified Pos007 cases reached terminal status. Invoke selection only after all intended production cases terminate, keep Pos008/refinement cases outside that directory, and independently verify the grid/specimen/source inventory. Missing exit records currently cause an error, but an absent whole config does not. Preserve SELECTION.json before test scoring and do not regenerate it after adding test records. An absent eligible family must be reported as missing, not silently replaced. Ties use the current enumeration/stable-sort order; record that order rather than claiming an unimplemented simpler-model tie rule.

## Practical success and numerical qualification

The frozen conjunction is appropriately more demanding than a favorable displacement number alone: at least 5% lower Pos008 equal-octant MSE than unchanged harmonic, no worse raw P1 strain NRMSE, no worse guarded local-average strain NRMSE, and all required numerical gates. Compare identical eligible nodes/tetrahedra/stencils and report all octants, failures and runtime. These are practical thresholds chosen after earlier baseline inspection, not significance levels or uncertainty-derived adequacy bounds.

`row.valid` currently means exit code zero and final converged step 10. The solver exits immediately on a failed step, so ordinary successful production completion implies prior steps converged under its fixed tolerance. Nevertheless **valid is not full mechanical qualification**: separately inspect each history for residual, determinant, finite diagnostics, slip KKT, dissipation and reaction-work closure; apply terminal 2% energy and selected 1% refinement limits; retain the exact-incremental-Hessian screen and its uncertainty. Missing or unresolved checks cannot become a qualified win. The row helper hardcodes ten steps, so it cannot classify 20-step controls as valid without a separate declared control reader; do not reinterpret that failure as physical nonconvergence.

The combined 20-step/302-direction control tests aggregate numerical sensitivity, but by changing both at once does not separately identify step and angular error; compensating errors are possible. Label this joint refinement honestly. A nonnegative 32-vector Lanczos subspace screen does not establish absence of every negative mode, global stability or uniqueness. Resolved negative modes invalidate stable-equilibrium claims as frozen.

## Interpretation boundary

Prescribed inner and outer displacement data supply the motion. Unknown boundary vectors receive modeled natural traction conditions. Absolute homogeneous stiffness is not identified by these displacement-driven fields. An assumed proportional path from a drug-relaxed reference does not establish a truly virgin material, observed loading history or measured plastic dissipation. Plastic lengthening remains an internal fiber-slip surrogate; no actual junction slip, measured graph connectivity, aging chemistry, cell force, fracture toughness or independent biological replication is identified. A successful numerical benchmark result remains conditional on these assumptions and unresolved DVC/reference/segmentation uncertainty.
