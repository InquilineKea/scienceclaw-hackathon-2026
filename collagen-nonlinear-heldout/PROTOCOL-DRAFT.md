# Nonlinear buckling and slip-surrogate benchmark extension

Prospective extension drafted 2026-10-31 before new nonlinear predictions. The prior Pos008 outcomes have already been inspected and motivated this extension. Therefore **Pos008 is a reused benchmark, not a new confirmatory holdout**, even if parameters are subsequently selected only on Pos007. This document fixes a finite candidate budget and practical success criterion before new outcomes; it cannot undo adaptive reuse of the benchmark. No compute, installation, spending or publication was performed to prepare this design.

## Question and scope

Can a continuum microplane law with compression softening, with or without incremental internal fiber slip, improve observed interior displacement prediction over the already competitive harmonic interpolator? The microplanes are continuum integration directions, not resolved measured fibers or junctions. Internal plastic strain is a **fiber-slip surrogate**, not observed junction sliding, molecular crosslink damage, fibril rupture or an identified remodeling mechanism. Endpoint pre/post images do not measure loading history, yielding, hysteresis, dissipation or residual set.

Reuse the exact reference images, measured coordinate convention, finite-validity rules, outer/near-cell supplied displacement nodes, support-purged score masks and eight physical octants from the prior benchmark. Source hashes and prior-result hashes must be recorded before implementation changes. Pos004 is implementation/training, Pos007 parameter selection, Pos008 final reused benchmark evaluation. Do not alter segmentation, score masks, model candidates or tolerance on the basis of new Pos008 errors. Independent specimen/batch status remains unknown. Bulk rheology and these field positions remain separate datasets.

## Finite candidate family and controls

Proposed maximum budget is **12 nonlinear constitutive candidates**, frozen before scoring. Use the same isotropic directional quadrature and common tension response in every candidate: external tension priors λs=0.04, ds=0.05 from the earlier bulk-selected scenario, explicitly not matched specimen calibration. Compression scale d0∈{0.0025,0.01,0.04}. Cross these three values with four slip states: no slip, or yield strain εy∈{0.01,0.03,0.10} with fixed positive hardening ratio 0.10. Dimensionless ratios and the exact yield/return-map equation must be specified in source before execution. No new concentration/scale fit is permitted from these displacement-controlled fields. If the implementation uses a different mathematically valid parameterization, replace this proposed grid prospectively with an explicit source-hashed grid; do not assert that these values map identically across inequivalent laws.

Every slip candidate has its exact no-slip counterpart at the same d0, tension response, geometry, quadrature, boundary ramp and solver settings. This supports matched ablation, rather than attributing a difference between unrelated best models to slip. The families have unequal search sizes (three buckling-only versus nine slip candidates); report this selection budget and avoid interpreting best-family differences as independent evidence for a mechanism. A full 12-case grid is the maximum, not a requirement to run unsuccessful or numerically inadmissible states repeatedly.

Prespecified baselines are the unchanged harmonic interpolation, prior selected linear elasticity ν=0, boundary-fitted affine and zero predictor. Include a linearized/no-softening constitutive limit as an implementation control if it is algebraically supported, without counting it as an additional tuned candidate. Full-fiber tension stiffening, compression softening and slip are distinct interventions; absent a separate tensile ablation, do not attribute all nonlinear gains solely to buckling.

## Loading history and solver discipline

The observed images provide endpoints. Use a declared assumed proportional ramp from zero boundary displacement to the measured endpoint, common to all candidates. Call ramp steps load fractions, not experimental time. Slip internal variables begin at zero, update by a specified irreversible return map, and persist only along that model's own ramp. Commit internal state only after a converged load step; rejected optimizer/line-search trial states must not irreversibly alter it. Do not reinitialize slip state at the endpoint to improve equilibrium. Specify tension/compression yield symmetry, hardening energy, flow sign and dissipation definition before running. If no thermodynamically consistent update is available, omit slip rather than imply mechanistic validation.

Use a fixed numerical rescue schedule and limit chosen on synthetic patch tests or Pos004 before selection/testing. At minimum commission: rigid translation/rotation or the declared small-strain equivalent; affine gradient/force finite differences; positive-energy admissible ranges; elastic/no-slip limit; nonnegative plastic dissipation; no artificial work generation; equal results under deterministic restart; and step refinement of final displacements/internal energy. A physically unstable or unresolved state is a numerical failure, not experimental evidence of tearing.

Suggested primary gates are complete proportional ramp, independently recomputed free-force residual ≤1e−7 in the documented relative normalization at every accepted increment, supplied boundary preservation, finite values, nonnegative dissipation within a frozen roundoff floor, and ≤2% relative work/energy closure including stored/hardening/dissipated terms where implemented. Compare 10 versus 20 load steps for selected candidates using the same boundary path; require ≤1% change in primary displacement RMSE and cap work where that comparison is physically defined. Freeze the exact work quadrature and energy normalization before use. A solver that cannot provide an appropriate energy diagnostic must disclose the missing gate and cannot receive the full qualified-mechanics label. Numerical tolerances may be replaced only before outcome scoring with a justified commissioning contract, never relaxed to make a candidate win.

Bound iteration counts and wall time per candidate before launch; log actual timing/hardware. A reasonable initial ceiling is 10 minutes per candidate and a fixed maximum number of converged-step retries, subject to the parent's actual resources/authorization. Timeouts/nonconvergence remain scored as failures of coverage, not removed favorable exclusions. This draft grants no cloud allocation. Harmonic's much smaller runtime is part of the practical comparison even if nonlinear errors fall.

## Selection, final comparison and practical beat criterion

On Pos007 choose the best numerically qualified buckling-only candidate and best numerically qualified slip candidate by **equal-weight mean of octant vector MSE**, with deterministic ties going to no slip, fewer free parameters, then the prelisted candidate order. Selection does not use strain error, Pos008 error or runtime to change the grid. Record all candidates, failures, source hashes and chosen parameters before new Pos008 scoring. Evaluate both selected candidates and their fixed matched no-slip ablations on Pos008. Do not evaluate the entire grid on Pos008 and retroactively name its best candidate the selected result.

The primary practical success rule on reused Pos008 is conjunctive:

1. At least **5% lower primary equal-octant vector MSE than the unchanged harmonic baseline**, using exactly the same valid score nodes and weights.
2. Secondary **volume-weighted infinitesimal-strain Frobenius MSE/NRMSE no worse than harmonic**, under the unchanged strain operator and eligible tetrahedra. This is a secondary safeguard, not an additional selection objective. It differs from the octant displacement weighting.
3. All prospective numerical gates pass and complete common outcome coverage is preserved. A candidate does not qualify by excluding hard nodes or tetrahedra. Runtime and any remaining measurement/numerical uncertainty are disclosed.

Using the already published baseline values only, the unchanged harmonic primary MSE is 1.1281372528 µm², so the fixed 5% target is ≤1.0717303902 µm². Its secondary strain Frobenius NRMSE is 0.7420726542. These thresholds were chosen after seeing the earlier baseline and before nonlinear results; they are practical engineering criteria, not significance levels, uncertainty-calibrated equivalence margins or proof of biological adequacy. No model claim follows if error differences are within unresolved numerical/measurement uncertainty. Show all eight octant contrasts, including worsening regions, rather than only a pooled win.

Leading outcomes are primary MSE in µm² and RMSE in µm. Secondary NRMSE uses the earlier outcome-norm convention after supplied-boundary-only translation removal, explicitly labeled observed-field normalized. Uniform stiffness can cancel from elastic displacement-boundary equilibrium; for nonlinear/plastic models, only dimensionless law ratios may affect displacement under proportional scale changes. Do not claim an independently identified absolute modulus or cell force. Unknown boundary vectors still imply natural zero-traction modeling, not known experimental free surfaces.

Passing this rule supports “improved prediction on an adaptively reused conditional benchmark.” It does not support a fresh held-out validation, identified slip mechanism, aging response, known tractions, true network topology or prediction within measured uncertainty. To test generalization, freeze the selected law and masks before a genuinely uninspected experimental field is extracted/scored.

## Fresh-field feasibility: metadata-only search, no downloads

The three Classic positions have already been used and cannot become fresh examples by relabeling their roles. A roughly 123 µm DynamicNK92 cube may not retain adequate interior after near-cell exclusion plus 35 µm DVC support purging; do not claim eligibility without counting geometry-only score blocks. The earlier NIST endpoint timed out; synthetic images are commissioning data, not a replacement experimental test.

Two public raw-image leads merit a bounded metadata audit:

- [Zenodo 17431762](https://zenodo.org/records/17431762), linked to the [2024 collagen hydrogel atlas](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actbio.2024.07.002), provides a 3.8 GB archive of three Leica files covering collagen concentrations 0.8/1.5/2.3 mg/ml, each with 9–12 individual cells at paired pre/post-inhibitor acquisitions. Public channels include cells, beads and white light. The landing page does not establish physical field size, depth, voxel spacing, batch independence or ready measured displacements; 35 µm guard feasibility remains unknown. It could provide genuinely new cells and a closer bulk/data provenance link if metadata and units support it, but it is not ready measured graph data. The dataset's published assembly/processing and assay conditions must be matched rather than assumed from a concentration label.
- [Zenodo 13773792](https://zenodo.org/records/13773792), cited by a [2025 3D TFM protocol](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41596-025-01275-0), lists paired 7.6 GB stressed/relaxed mosaic ND2 files and two 4.1/4.6 GB nonmosaic LIF files, 24 GB total. Potentially wider lateral coverage does not guarantee adequate depth or independent interior blocks. Exact field geometry, channel pairing, licensing and identity need inspection before download/extraction or new validation claims.

Neither lead was downloaded for this draft. Public raw-image availability does not authorize executing format readers locally; apply the existing software security policy. Prefer read-only metadata/header inventory first, avoid duplicate downloads coordinated with the parent, and keep evaluated inverse forces out of the ground truth. If no new field passes the geometry/uncertainty gates, publish the benchmark reuse honestly and leave external validation pending.
