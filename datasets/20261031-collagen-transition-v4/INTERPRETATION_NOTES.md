# Interpretation rules for this exact pilot

- Connectivity is the realized mean graph degree, not a direct conversion from three-dimensional collagen-gel measurements. The graph Laplacian is not the mechanical Hessian.
- Nonaffinity is translation-subtracted incremental displacement. Weak modes, solver choice and strain steps may affect peaks. No critical-exponent fit or universal critical strain is supported by this pilot alone.
- The locking sweep fixes each graph and its initial spring constants. An independent readback checks whether the saved pre-hold stress curves are actually identical. Locking changes a declared plastic mobility law, not measured AGE concentration.
- Plasticity and rupture are path dependent. Preserve every invalid sample. Restrict headline comparisons to suitable converged histories and disclose unsaved intermediate hold diagnostics.
- Disk number and attachment are fixed at one disk per network junction; area fraction changes radii. Initial overlaps/prestress are possible and are recorded. Compression reaction is first-Piola stress in model units, not hydrostatic pressure.
- The helix-coil half-folded point follows the imposed site-stabilization assumptions. It is a model demonstration, not an independent prediction or molecular-dynamics result. Real thermal MD remains unperformed.
- The three-stage LOX model is a new mathematical sensitivity model, not a fit to the earlier H5 loop or experimental signaling. Stable roots and hysteresis only establish properties of those declared equations.
- The separate matched-stiffness study motivates stricter numerical interpretation but its effect sizes and refinements do not validate this new model.
