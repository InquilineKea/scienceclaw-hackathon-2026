# Independent reader and conditional forward-mechanics review

2026-10-31. The inspected implementation is the original `read-data.py` and the current prospective protocol. This audit uses Python standard libraries to inspect archive names, NPY/TIFF headers and PIV coordinates only. It executes no Saenopy/package code, unpickles no array, fits no model and computes no experimental displacement error. Pixel intensities and numerical displacement values were not scored. Metadata observations are in CLASSIC-METADATA-ONLY.json, CLASSIC-PIV-GEOMETRY-ONLY.json and CLASSIC-TIFF-TAGS.json.

## Reader findings

The NPY reader is inert: `ast.literal_eval` handles the header, dtypes are explicitly numeric, and object/pickled arrays are rejected. Its two-dimensional Fortran-order indexing `a[i+j*N]` is correct and essential: the Classic evaluated displacement arrays are shape14400×3 with `fortran_order=True`. Classic node and tetrahedron arrays are C-order. The three explicit evaluated files correspond to positions004/007/008; selecting an arbitrary first archive member is not a valid specimen-selection rule.

Before use, require supported complete NPY version tuples, minimum header/body lengths, boolean fortran_order, nonnegative integer shape, exact byte count and the expected Nx3 or Mx4 array shape. Arrays with dimension>2 are currently returned flat rather than reconstructed; callers must not silently treat that as the declared n-dimensional shape. Finite coordinates, integer in-range tetrahedron indices, repeated-node rejection and nondegenerate physical volumes are required. NPY row ordering must remain matched across positions and displacements. Sorting nodes requires the same permutation of displacements and the inverse permutation of tetrahedron indices; never reshape by an assumed xyz loop order alone.

The current displacement JSON uses `allow_nan=True`, which emits non-standard JSON and does not make missingness explicit. Retain original NPY bytes, serialize nonfinite measurements as null with an explicit finite/quality mask, and never substitute zero motion. Exclusion rules must be frozen from measurement quality/support before candidate prediction errors are viewed.

There was no TIFF decoder in `read-data.py` at the audit snapshot. The Classic ZIP's first `.tif` is actually a PNG RGBA LUT thumbnail,200×500, named Pos008_S001ch2LUT.tif. Select exact z-index/channel slice patterns and verify file magic. Actual slices are512×512 unsigned8-bit, uncompressed TIFF6, with four strips of128rows; StripOffsets/StripByteCounts arrays are out-of-line. The first IFD is at byte262152, after image data. Thus a first4KB/one-strip-only implementation would be wrong. Support both endian conventions correctly or fail closed; reject unsupported BigTIFF/compression/sample layouts instead of guessing. Bound all offsets/counts, check assembled pixel count, tag orientation and next-IFD status, and record the channel/axis map. TIFF nominal resolution fields do not establish the independently verified physical xyz voxel spacing.

## Geometry-only eligibility

The organoid PIV geometry is24×24×3 with depth68.50µm. A35µm guard from both top/bottom boundaries already consumes the depth, even before an inner inclusion boundary is imposed. It cannot pass the proposed three-dimensional separated-interior validation gate. Do not shrink the guard after inspecting outcomes.

Classic positions004/007/008 each have24×24×25 PIV coordinates,14,400nodes and76,176tetrahedra. The PIV-center spans are353.820×353.820×356.628µm, with spacings15.3835/15.3835/14.8595µm. The measured-vector domain differs from the nominal raw image box; use the former for the actual boundary-conditioned FE subdomain. The35µm interrogation support exceeds twice the largest vector spacing (~30.77µm).

For a two-node supplied outer shell, measure the guard from the **innermost supplied vector supports**, not from the raw-image edge. For every supplied inner-boundary vector, guard scored vectors against interrogation-window overlap. A35µm Euclidean center distance does not guarantee that two35µm axis-aligned cubic windows are disjoint: a25/25/0µm offset still overlaps. Reject a scored center if its coordinate differences from any supplied boundary center are all less than the declared window side lengths (or use a conservatively sufficient Euclidean distance>=sqrt(3)*35µm). Freeze whether this rule means direct non-overlap or an additional empty support-width gap. Apply it identically to all models, train/test sectors and strain stencils. Count surviving spatial blocks before displacement values are scored.

Cell/inclusion center, shape and domain intersection must come from independent metadata/reference images. If a reliable cell surface cannot be segmented, a prospectively specified observation cut enclosing the whole imaged cell can define an inner Dirichlet boundary, but name it an artificial measured-motion cut. It is not measured collagen junction topology or a reconstructed cell interface. Do not choose its radius/center to minimize prediction errors. Three imaging positions are not automatically three independent biological specimens or batches.

## Mechanical baseline and identifiability

An original linear tetrahedral elastic implementation is an appropriate mechanical baseline for a conditional displacement-field test. Its tetrahedral grid is a mechanical sampling/discretization mesh, not measured fiber connectivity. Boundary displacements are inputs and cannot also be scored as predictions. Interior body forces are assumed zero in the scored material domain; the chosen inner cut must enclose the force-generating cell/cavity.

With homogeneous linear elasticity and fully supplied inner/outer Dirichlet motion, a uniform modulus multiplier cancels from `Kff u_f = -Kfb g_b`. Therefore interior displacement errors cannot validate the absolute bovine stiffness scale. They can test constitutive shape/Poisson ratio, geometry, heterogeneity or anisotropy under the same supplied boundary motion. A single quasistatic pair does not validate G″ or temporal relaxation. Freeze Poisson ratio independently or report it as an assumed sensitivity; shear rheology alone does not identify it. Linear P1 displacement-only tetrahedra can volumetrically lock nearν=.5; do not interpret a nearly incompressible sensitivity as a numerically controlled material result without the appropriate mixed formulation/convergence evidence.

For each tetrahedron, set D=[x1−x0,x2−x0,x3−x0], V=|detD|/6. Shape gradients for vertices1–3 are rows of D^-1; vertex0 is their negative sum. For displacement gradient H=sum_a u_a⊗gradNa, ε=(H+Hᵀ)/2, σ=λ trε I+2με. The matrix-free internal vector is f_a=V σ gradNa, and energy is E=V[λ(trε)^2/2+μ ε:ε]. The Jacobi contribution for node a/component i is V[μ|gradNa|²+(λ+μ)(gradNa_i)^2]. Require μ>0 and bulk modulusλ+2μ/3>0.

Apply Dirichlet elimination symmetrically: u=g+v with v=0 on the supplied boundary; solve the free block for v. Overwriting constrained rows while retaining their columns creates a nonsymmetric operator unsuitable for ordinary conjugate gradients. Validate free residual and imposed-value accuracy independently of the iterative solver's success flag. Disconnected components without sufficient noncollinear constraints are underdetermined; an artificial spring is not a legitimate hidden repair.

## Independent numerical commissioning before experimental scoring

1. **Canonical tetrahedron.** For vertices0,e1,e2,e3, V=1/6 and gradients(-1,-1,-1),e1,e2,e3. For u_x=γy, energy=Vμγ²/2; f0=V(-μγ,-μγ,0),f1=V(0,μγ,0),f2=V(μγ,0,0),f3=0. This catches gradient transpose, shear-factor and volume errors.
2. **General affine patch.** Use a non-axis-aligned tetrahedron and a known full3×3 affine displacement gradient, including symmetric and skew terms. Recovered H must be exact; energy must equal the analytic homogeneous value. On a conforming3×3×3-node cube, prescribe affine displacements on all outer nodes and require the center's assembled free residual to vanish and CG to recover its exact affine value. Test both node permutations/orientations and an independent tetrahedral subdivision.
3. **Rigid modes and covariance.** Uniform translation and infinitesimal rigid rotation must give zero strain/energy/internal vector. Rotating both coordinates and displacement vectors by the same orthogonal basis transformation must preserve energy and rotate forces. A finite90° rigid displacement does not have zero infinitesimal strain; do not claim finite-rotation objectivity for this linear law.
4. **Symmetry, energy and derivatives.** Check p·Kq=q·Kp, nonnegative u·Ku, E=u·Ku/2, and central finite differences of energy versus the assembled internal vector. Compare matrix-free action and Jacobi diagonal to a separately assembled small dense reference. Include a cut-out cavity mesh and disconnected-component detection.
5. **Dirichlet elimination.** Check exact boundary values, free residual, zero-input solution, uniform-translation boundary recovery and energy/work equality with boundary reactions. Use identical boundary masks/values for harmonic and mechanical comparators; the harmonic result remains a kinematic baseline.
6. **Manufactured nonaffine solution.** On a cube with zero boundary motion, use u=(x(1−x)y(1−y)z(1−z),0,0) and its analytic body force −μ∇²u−(λ+μ)∇divu solely for numerical commissioning. Demonstrate decreasing displacement/strain errors under actual FE refinement. Experimental scoring still has no inferred interior nodal force.
7. **Units and mesh sensitivity.** Consistent scaling x→Lx,u→Lu preserves strain, scales energy by L³ and forces by L². Refine the same physical domain and boundary functions; a changed cavity cut, changed measurement sampling or altered BC support is a sensitivity analysis, not pure mesh convergence. Refined boundary values must interpolate only supplied boundary measurements, never withheld interior vectors.

Freeze numerical tolerances and admissible coverage from these commissioning cases before experimental scores. Failures remain in the denominator. Source/package implementation checks cannot establish material adequacy.

## Scoring boundary

Primary scores should use block-mean vector displacement errors and component RMSE under identical masks, with block rather than vector replication. Keep physical µm units and a training-only normalization scale. Local strain is secondary: apply the same prespecified >=measurement-support averaging/gradient operator to predictions and observations, and score only stencils whose full measurement support remains in held-out eligible material. Radial means alone can hide anisotropic errors. No biological p-value or independent frequency/aging/species validation follows from this conditional field test.
