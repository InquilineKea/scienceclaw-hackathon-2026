## Questions and distinct estimands

1. Can one shared material law, fitted only to designated training rheology, predict **whole held-out rheology files/batches**: G′, G″ and nonlinear stress/stiffening response?
2. Given independently measured boundary motion and reference geometry, can an independently specified forward law predict **withheld 3D displacements** around a contracting inclusion? Can it predict coarse-grained local strains at the measurement resolution?
3. Does preserving measured orientation/geometry improve that local prediction relative to a simple continuum baseline, after controlling boundary conditions and overall stiffness? Connectivity may only be called measured if images actually resolve junction topology; crossings in a projection or intensity ridges are not proof of bonded junctions.

Bulk and local datasets lacking a shared gel/specimen/batch identifier answer separate questions. They do not jointly validate one specimen-specific multiscale material model. A successful field reconstruction under a fitted inverse-force model is not the same as forward mechanical validation. Aging, rupture, molecular chemistry and species rankings are outside this initial contract.

Example material parameters are tutorial inputs, not newly validated material constants. An evaluated .saenopy file may contain inverse displacements/forces and embedded model assumptions; it is not independent force ground truth. Brightfield projections and 2D orientation examples are not measured 3D fiber topology.

For either candidate, record SHA-256 hashes of retained raw files, metadata/README and exact preprocessing version. Download raw images as data only after bounded archive inspection. Do not execute bundled scripts or deserialize executable/pickled content. Before model fitting, classify readiness independently for: paired 3D images; physical axes/scale; image-derived displacement; image-derived architecture; known boundary motion; known force; independent rheology; shared specimen identity. A missing item stays missing rather than being inferred from a convenient tutorial default.

**Minimum forward-field gate:** independently observable reference geometry, three physical axes/scales, paired states, and a sufficiently observed inclusion/domain displacement boundary. If loading or displacement boundary is unknown, stop absolute forward stress/force validation. A defensible fallback is a clearly named conditional shape/decay or blocked field-prediction test with the required driving amplitude estimated from training regions only. These are aggregate curves with reported spread, not recovered replicate-level samples. Source 22 batch-A G′/G″ can support training, but **no untouched frequency batch has yet been identified**, so held-out G′/G″ validation remains unavailable unless an additional independent source is frozen before scoring. Organoid batch C must not inform constitutive fitting. A shared batch label does not itself establish same-gel/specimen identity; verify that separately. This amendment records the curator's metadata-only report and must be checked against the owning files before execution, without changing the split based on observed errors.

**Bulk rheology:** fit shared parameters on complete training specimens/batches; hold out entire independent batch when identifiers permit. If only filenames are available, use whole-file holdout and explicitly state that independence across files is unverified. Do not split frequencies from one sweep across train/test. At least one untouched final test group is required after choosing the constitutive family. If too few groups exist for separate family selection and final testing, freeze the family in advance or report nested leave-group-out predictions as cross-validation, never as an untouched test. The earlier bulk model-selection results are prior information, not fresh independent validation. Hyperparameters, parameter bounds, frequency/amplitude windows, weighting and exclusion rules must be frozen with the split.

**Local fields:** prioritize held-out whole experimental inclusions/specimens if there are several independently identified ones. Do not assume three positions are three biological specimens. With one specimen, use prespecified spatially blocked prediction and call it internal within-specimen validation. Make geometry-defined angular sectors as the primary split (for example, eight azimuthal sectors about a metadata-defined axis, alternating four training/four test), with guard bands at least one final DVC interrogation-window support and never less than two field-vector spacings. The exact sector origin and guard rule must be fixed before displacement fitting. If geometry makes azimuth undefined, freeze a Cartesian block partition from the reference bounding box instead. Report an a priori radial outer-shell extrapolation separately; it answers a different question and is especially boundary-sensitive.

Never randomly assign neighboring vectors/voxels. DVC windows overlap, strains are differentiated from correlated displacements, and neighboring fibers share load. Field vectors and time frames do not increase independent specimen count. Whole time courses stay grouped. Any temporal extrapolation also requires frozen loading/drift definitions.

**Proposed conditional example mode, pending reference-only geometry audit:** the curator reports one organoid stack pair with 52 planes per state and a 12³-vector evaluated PIV grid. Image physical dimensions are approximately 739×739×103 µm, so this is a finite thin 3D slab, not an infinite isotropic spherical field. Verify actual PIV coordinates rather than assuming isotropic spacing from the 12³ index shape. A two-node outer boundary shell and independently image-segmented inclusion boundary may provide measured Dirichlet conditions, with interior nodes held out. This tests interior prediction conditional on both near and far observed motion; it does not validate a driving force or independently predict far-field propagation. The boundary vectors are inputs and cannot also be scored as predictions.

Freeze boundary/test node indices before loading their displacement values, using reference intensities, physical coordinates and DVC support metadata only. Do not choose a central 4³ cavity or a 120 µm cylinder as if it were the observed organoid without checking reference geometry. If the inclusion spans the thin z domain, model its actual intersection with the domain and disclose missing/unobservable surfaces. Retain all outer surfaces consistently in each baseline, including top/bottom truncation. Reserve a physical guard of at least one DVC support between supplied boundary vectors/training vectors and scored interior blocks wherever feasible. Count surviving eligible blocks and independent spatial support lengths before outcome inspection. If the cavity, outer shell and guard consume the domain or leave no separated interior blocks, report the blocked-validation gate failed; do not shrink masks after seeing errors. A sensitivity run with a smaller guard can be descriptive but cannot replace that failed primary test.

For this boundary-conditioned mode, zero motion and training-fitted affine motion are descriptive reference predictors; vector-harmonic extension is a kinematic interpolation baseline, not a collagen material law. Linear elastic finite-domain FE is the mechanical baseline. A positive, image-orientation-weighted elliptic extension can test a measured-covariate interpolation hypothesis but should not be called calibrated fiber mechanics without an explicit constitutive derivation. Give every applicable model the identical geometry, boundary values and score mask. Distinguish this internal spatial test from the independent bulk batch-C test throughout.

## Measurement pipeline and uncertainty

Infer architecture from the **reference** stack, before inspecting held-out deformation. Correct axes and anisotropic voxel sizes; document PSF/optical-depth limitations, cropping, missing z coverage, skeletonization, branch/junction ambiguity and boundary truncation. Preserve measured orientation and segment-size distributions only to the image's actual resolution; report unresolved substructure. If connectivity requires an assumption, compare a declared connectivity ensemble rather than claiming exact measured graph recovery.

DVC/PIV preprocessing, drift correction, window size, overlap, confidence mask and displacement regularization are measurement choices. Choose them on separate calibration data or raw-image quality diagnostics, not by whichever choice makes a mechanics law win. Synthetic S2 with known motion and known image shifts can estimate bias/resolution; repeated unloaded images, if available, estimate experimental noise. Neither perfectly characterizes real remodeling, occlusion or non-affine texture changes. Preserve low-confidence regions and exclusion counts.

Use displacement as the primary observed field; local strain is a secondary derivative with larger, spatially correlated error. Compare predictions after applying the same effective spatial averaging/resolution as the measured field. Fix the strain operator and smoothing before held-out comparison. For large displacements use a specified finite-strain tensor and coordinate convention; do not silently compare engineering strain with Green–Lagrange strain. Track rigid drift separately from contraction; do not remove physical non-rigid motion as “drift.” Propagate plausible registration/window/segmentation/boundary choices as sensitivity envelopes rather than fictitious independent samples.

## Forward models and prevention of inverse circularity

Freeze no more than three primary predictors before fitting:

- **B0:** linear isotropic continuum with independently measured displacement boundary and the same outer-domain geometry. A spherical infinite-medium radial displacement proportional to 1/r² is only an analytic diagnostic when the source is approximately spherical, contraction is radial, observation is genuinely 3D, and boundaries are sufficiently remote. It is not a generic cell-force Green function or a universal collagen law. Fix amplitude from the measured inclusion displacement, or estimate it solely in training blocks and label that conditional.
- **B1:** strain-stiffening/stretch-buckling fiber or continuum law, same geometry/loading and training budget. Parameters come from independent matched rheology where available; otherwise fit only designated training blocks and report non-identifiability.
- **B2:** reference-image-informed anisotropic/fiber law with explicit orientation input. Keep global scale and loading controls aligned with B1; morphology is a measured covariate, not a freely tuned field fitted to test displacements. Use the same parameter-estimation budget and fixed segmentation alternatives.

Do not use Saenopy-inferred cell forces as independent inputs and then compare the same model's recovered displacements to claim validation. If inverse loading estimation is necessary, estimate it from an **independent boundary measurement** or a prespecified reduced loading family on training regions only; never assign enough free nodal forces to interpolate the full observed field. Apply exactly the same inferred loading parameters to each candidate law where physically meaningful, and disclose model dependence. A joint inverse fit of loading and modulus has scale ambiguities; unknown force plus displacement can leave absolute stiffness unidentified. Fitting each model an arbitrary force field destroys the proposed comparison.

## Frozen outcomes, scoring and adequacy criteria

Primary bulk outcomes are paired file-level prediction errors for G′ and G″ with shared fitting choices and physically meaningful units. Report each channel separately; a near-zero G″ makes relative error unstable, so choose measurement-noise floors from assay metadata/calibration before scoring. Include all prespecified frequencies/amplitudes, uncertainty and fit-window coverage. No selection of only the attractive nonlinear regime.

Primary local score is held-out block-mean squared vector-displacement error, averaged with equal weight over prespecified eligible blocks within each specimen. Also report vector RMSE in µm and each component; normalize by a training-only displacement scale or independently measured boundary amplitude, never by each model's own prediction. Secondary outcomes: radial/tangential displacement profiles, directional anisotropy, coarse-grained strain tensor errors and distance-dependent residuals. Report coverage and error for excluded/low-signal domains separately where feasible. A radial average alone can conceal a wrong anisotropic field.

Rank models by paired held-out specimen scores when genuinely independent specimens exist; within one specimen use paired block differences descriptively and show all block signs. A bootstrap of field vectors is prohibited. Whole-specimen intervals require enough independent specimens; spatial block resampling can describe within-field sensitivity but cannot create biological replication. Do not claim a powered significance test from a handful of public examples.

Numerical commissioning must establish equilibrium residuals, mesh/step convergence, imposed-boundary accuracy, rigid-motion/objectivity checks and work/energy consistency using reviewed solvers. Freeze tolerances from commissioning before experimental scoring. Require both models to have adequate numerical coverage; failed predictions are retained rather than removed from the denominator. Better error than B0 is a comparative result, not proof of experimental adequacy. Adequacy requires errors compatible with independently estimated measurement/boundary uncertainty; if that uncertainty cannot be established, report error magnitude without a pass/fail validation claim. Model selection uses training/nested validation only; keep final test scores untouched until the frozen pipeline is ready.

## Outputs and allowed conclusions

Publish a compact readiness table, frozen split/source hashes, raw-to-field measurement uncertainty, all model scores including failures, measured-versus-predicted 3D field slices and quiver plots, residual maps, radial/anisotropic profiles and optional animation with measured/predicted panels under identical scale. Label a movie's time axis as actual acquisition time or quasistatic state progression as appropriate. Cite public sources and distinguish raw measurements from derived fields, inferred forces and simulated responses.

Allowed initial claim if all gates pass: “The frozen law predicted withheld bulk curves and/or withheld spatial displacement regions under stated measurement, loading and independence conditions.” Without shared specimen identity, explicitly write that bulk and local tests are separate datasets. Without independently known loading, limit claims to conditional spatial prediction. Without resolved junctions, say image-informed architecture rather than measured connectivity. Do not describe this study as validated aging chemistry, fracture toughness, organism robustness or independent biological replication when those measurements were not made.
