Any image-orientation model is secondary unless its coefficients/algorithm are frozen before test scoring.

For homogeneous linear elasticity under prescribed displacements and zero unknown-boundary traction, a uniform stiffness multiplier cancels from the displacement equation. Therefore these spatial tests **cannot identify or validate absolute modulus k0, validate the rheology scale, or determine cell forces**. Differences can test Poisson-ratio-dependent field shape or interpolation assumptions under these boundaries. Inner measured displacements are inputs rather than independent predictions of cell-generated contraction. Supplied boundary data are never included in the error score. Do not use evaluated inverse forces or optimized tractions as ground truth.

Keep the bulk batch-summary test separate from internal spatial prediction. No same-specimen or same-batch mechanical identity is established between these Classic examples and the rheology source. No measured collagen graph connectivity, aging law, fracture toughness, Gc, molecular crosslink calibration or organism robustness is validated by this protocol.
