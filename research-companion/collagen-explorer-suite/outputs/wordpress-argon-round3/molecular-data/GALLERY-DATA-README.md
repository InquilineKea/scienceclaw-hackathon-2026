# Curated collagen dashboard data

The `gallery-ready/` folder contains 16 standalone JSON datasets, with 125 actual-coordinate models and 4,356 saved frames. The payload total is 28,342,338 bytes; `gallery-ready/inventory.json` records each file's exact SHA256, size, model/frame count, table row count, qualification and curated plot defaults. `gallery-ready/validation.json` records row/column equality against the original extracted tables and structural checks. The private extraction manifests in this parent folder retain local artifact provenance; gallery JSONs contain no `/Users/` host paths.

`ready/` preserves the fuller extracted data schema; the normalized files remove duplicate geometry/molecule/spatial containers and expose `models` for the gallery. `normalize_gallery.py` is our own Python-standard-library transformation. No recovered source code, package installer, model, simulation or imported script was executed during extraction or normalization.

## Renderer contract

Each model has `id`, `label`, `nodes`, `edges`, `units`, `evidence` and `frames`. Optional `groups`, `labels`, `elements`, `atomic_numbers`, `recordBinding`, `referenceNodes`, `forces` and `forceUnits` retain saved semantics. Edges are two-index adjacency; molecular bond orders are not a claim of three-dimensional crosslink mechanics. Reference coordinates are shown without invented alignment or interpolation.

A frame contains `nodes` and a `label`. Actual `time_ps` becomes `time` with `timeUnits=ps`. Four-bead trajectories use explicitly nominal CG time, not physical kinetics. Short-crosslink frames have only saved frame indices because their export lacks timestamps. Do not assign a time from the frame number. Models with no saved frames have an empty array, not synthetic animation.

Force entries may be `null`. These mean no force was measured/exported for that node. They must be omitted from force glyphs, tooltip quantities, normalization and legend ranges. They are never zeros. Only MACE final atom forces and coupled network attachment-site forces are exported. There are no measured forces on the coupled reference fibers.

`table.qualification` is either null or `{mode:"all_true",columns:[...],missing:"unqualified",label,scope}`. Columns are semantically relevant saved boolean gates. A condition such as `switch`/`tight`, a count of passing states or a scalar residual must not be used as a generic qualification flag. A saved numerical/runtime check passing does not establish biological/chemical validation.

Four-bead `mappingGuides` illustrate the projection from nine Cα atoms per CG centroid. They are not chemical bonds or crosslinks. The original chain and CG adjacency edges are in `edges`. Four-bead mapping models are exact time-zero projections; independent saved CG fold trajectories are separate models.

Coupled-law models include all 24 saved reference meshes and four selected matched AIMNet m0/r0 examples: seed 29, parent_z 3.2, narrow/broad × diagonal/full coupled. Their reference fibers remain undeformed, while their 15 deformed attachment sites and 15 site force vectors are the exact saved outputs. No fiber deformation or fiber-node forces should be inferred. All 648 scalar network cases and 17,496 conditional signal rows remain available in tables.

## Scientific boundaries by dataset

- `reference-1dzi` and `reference-1k6f`: actual crystal-reference Cα/ion geometry. 1DZI contains engineered collagen peptides bound to an integrin I-domain. These are not simulated lesions, affinity measurements, or full-length canonical collagen mappings.
- `carbonyl-scenarios`: saved assumed-input kinetics and terminal scenario quantiles. Unknown empirical rates remain null. Terminal quantiles do not provide time-dependent uncertainty bands. Contact candidates use the fixed 1DZI reference and do not establish lesion effects.
- `coupled-3d-law`: exact saved model geometry/scalars. Four of six AIMNet derivative pairs and one of three classical-dry pairs were admitted; no wet pair was admitted. Cell/YAP quantities remain assumed response surrogates, not calibrated signaling predictions. Paired broad-minus-narrow tables are labeled arithmetic differences from exact matched saved cases.
- `wet-transfer-gates`: newer wet derivative controls remain unsuccessful (0/24 factorial passes, no admitted wet law or new 3D network). Passing fixed-pose Ewald checks do not repair the failed transfer-law gates. Historical executed wet states and current transfer rejection are distinct.
- `mace-fragments`: eight saved neutral minimal-fragment relaxations plus actual scalar scans and stored atom forces. Nonreactive force peaks are not rupture forces. Fragment convergence is not native crosslink/fibril validation or a tissue modulus calibration.
- `snt-ligand-ensemble`: 36 independent 20-ps ligand pilots; final geometries exist for all 36, but full XYZ frames exist only for the 12 replica-zero trajectories. Dielectric screening ε=78.4 is not explicit or implicit aqueous solvent. There is no enzyme, LOXL2, Cu/LTQ or reactive chemistry. Replicate SD and within-run fluctuation are distinct.
- `hydroxylation-prestress`: 12 short explicit-water peptide trajectories with actual proline/Hyp rings and retained hydroxyl oxygens; atom-derived puckering checks match archived means. The 216 prestress-network rows are an independent normalized model, not calibrated by these short MD runs.
- `four-bead-calibration`: exact saved leave-one-replica-out distributions, common-bin training/held-out/CG histograms, and actual coordinate projections/CG samples. All 180 moment checks match saved validation rows within 1e-5. Correlated frames are not independent replicate confidence intervals. A tiny four-bead peptide segment is not a fibril or a validated modulus/fracture law.
- `comparative-evidence-design`: source-specific mammal/skin observations remain distinct from proposed L9 experiments and subjective species triage. Pentosidine quadratic accumulation coefficients are not instantaneous rates, and perforation endpoints are not tensile moduli. Missingness is not zero.
- `short-crosslink-md`: twelve 50-ps runtime/geometry pilots with 30-ps free tails. Distinct glucosepane/dityrosine setups cannot support a cross-chemistry stiffness ranking. All displayed frames are actual saved heavy-atom coordinates; timestamps are unavailable.
- `decorin-collagen-gag`: prepared arbitrary encounter seed, not a validated binding pose or a post-dynamics structure. Protein Cα and glycan residue-centroid projection omits solvent. Reported heavy-atom separation values are source values, not recomputed from the display projection.
- `hne-candidate-precision`: current 1-Nov repair supersedes the historical failed periodic gate. Matched-precision diagnostics isolate sensitivity and six final periodic configurations pass unchanged numerical gates. The 210/160-ps adduct/control sampling is single-seed and chemically unvalidated; the models are small Lys(HNE) candidate/control starting geometries, not collagen or tissue. No stereochemical, mechanism or cell-force calibration claim follows.
- `martini-pulling-virial`: source-reported force/pressure-convention checks and a 15-ps numerical smoke test. No full benchmark reproduction or new fibril trajectory is implied. Its conceptual relations carry no invented spatial coordinates.
- `cross-scale-provenance` and `study-design-and-gates`: categorical provenance/experimental proposals and explicit transfer gates. No invented numerical time series, mechanistic fit or observed three-dimensional geometry.

## Final default-view QA

`default-view-qa.json` verifies every configured x/y/color exists in its selected table and every filter combination selects rows. All 15 configured defaults pass; 1K6F is geometry-only. The wet default now isolates one control pose, replica 0, original PME tolerance and grip-rotation direction, comparing the two LJ switch conditions over eight stored rows. Angular step is radians; derivative error is kJ/mol/radian. Martini defaults to the three pressure shifts in bar; its force, length, virial and energy quantities are excluded from that numeric axis. HNE intentionally uses a categorical runtime axis for the matched mixed/double precision comparison. The two source-evidence/proposal defaults use `plotKind=matrix`. No raw scientific table value changed during this QA.
