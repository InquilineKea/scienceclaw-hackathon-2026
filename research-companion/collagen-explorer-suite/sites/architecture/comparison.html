Interpretation: This is a paired synthetic-network experiment, not a validated tendon prediction. A higher curve means greater stress under the same constrained x strain in this model; it does not establish greater toughness, resilience or biological quality. Connectivity does not prove rigidity. Discretization convergence and specimen-specific calibration remain open.

Both cases have fixed transverse dimensions: F = diag(1 + strain, 1, 1). One-percent or smaller load increments, up to 12,000 optimizer iterations per load, force criterion 2×10⁻⁶ max(1, EA). Only strains accepted by both cases can be compared with the shared slider. Missing or unresolved loads are never filled by extrapolation. Small stresses may be sensitive to the force tolerance and optimizer path.

The top-20% energy statistic is a share of axial stretching energy, not a fraction of tissue force or total macroscopic stress. Per-fiber ranking uses peak absolute axial segment force. Fiber numbers are local to each case; compare endpoint pairs when matching shared fibers. Neither includes a rupture, contact, sliding or damage law. Changing layouts changes more than sparsity; use Copy A to B and keep positions/seed fixed for a paired sparsity comparison.

Fiber bending, natural crimp and the existing regularized twist term are retained. Twist rigidity is assumed to be half the bending rigidity. No molecular binding or crosslink chemistry is inferred. Download source, tests and numerical results · Methods and limitations on WordPress
