What was actually withheld?

Position007 selected12 parameter combinations. Position008 was examined in the preceding study and is reused here. Valid near-cell and outer-face motion is supplied; guarded interior motion is predicted. The4355 vectors are correlated observations within one position.

  1. Keep the same inputs. Same measured boundary values, frozen score masks and missing-vector handling.
  2. Compare additional mechanics. Compression softening and irreversible lengthening, with a matched nonlinear control.
  3. Test the numerical margin. At least5% lower vector MSE than harmonic, neither strain score worse, and all numerical gates passing.
Read the scientific limits

The mesh samples PIV motion; it is not a measured fiber graph. Absolute stiffness cancels in the homogeneous displacement-conditioned model. The loading history is an assumed proportional ramp from a drug-relaxed reference. Sliding is a plastic-lengthening surrogate, not observed junction motion.

Raw strains differentiate an approximately15µm sampling grid beneath a35µm correlation window. Averaged strain uses303 complete guarded5³ fits spanning about61×61×59µm. Both derive from the same displacement observations, not independent strain measurements. No chemical aging, species, fracture or toughness validation follows.
