The network atlas still uses hypothetical dimensionless parameters. No beaver multiplier, human multiplier or age-dependent rupture threshold is inferred from these backbones. Fixed-topology harmonic molecular dynamics cannot by itself predict covalent bond rupture.

The next defined experiment is a paired species × damage study: matched unmodified controls and one explicitly modeled AGE bridge, with the same hydration, temperature and post-translational-modification assumptions. AGEs-ColBuilder provides a chemistry-aware starting point; force-field compatibility, attachment sites and topology must be checked before combining it with CDSM. The large published fibril study is not reproduced by this pilot.

CDSM's backbone propensity lookup internally treats Y-position proline like hydroxyproline. That prevents this stage from serving as a clean hydroxylation-chemistry experiment. Native PTMs, tissue architecture and damage burden need separate evidence.
