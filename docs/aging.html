Reading brittleness: a higher stiffness ratio together with lower failure-strain or work ratios indicates a stiffness–extensibility tradeoff in this model. Check both metrics and numerical flags; higher stiffness alone is not evidence of greater toughness. Energy before change: choose work before first bond damage for the last intact sample, or first collagen damage to allow earlier connector rupture. These are mechanical damage events, not thermodynamic phase changes. Work includes recoverable elastic energy and is not dissipation alone.

A metric is unresolved if the 480/960-step difference is at least 5%, either solve is invalid, or (for failure work/strain) the stopping criteria disagree or a strain cap is reached. All selected seeds must pass; hollow points are retained for transparency without connecting lines. Before-damage work is sampled at the last intact step, with onset brackets in the download. Ratios also require a resolved paired zero-added baseline.

05 / What this says about aging

PRIMARY STUDIES · HYPOTHESES · LIMITS
EXPERIMENTAL CONTEXT

Restricting sliding can change damage.

Fessel and colleagues found glycation altered molecular sliding and damage mechanisms. Scale matters: tissue changes do not imply an identical change in fibril stiffness.

Fessel et al., 2014 ↗
A COUNTEREXAMPLE

Stiffer does not always mean brittle.

Svensson and colleagues observed stronger, stiffer glycated rat-tendon fibrils without induced brittleness. This explorer therefore does not force added connectors to lower collagen failure strain.

Svensson et al., 2018 ↗
MODELING CONTEXT

Density and bond properties both matter.

Kamml and colleagues varied AGE properties in a molecular-scale simulation. That motivates independent controls here; its parameters and molecular predictions are not transferred into this network model.

Kamml et al., 2023 ↗

Added springs are effective constraints between existing network nodes, not resolved glucosepane, pentosidine or enzymatic crosslink molecules. They change connectivity and add a separate elastic energy contribution without adding collagen fiber volume. The model has no molecular sliding coordinate, age kinetics, turnover, hydration, cells, viscoelastic time dependence or clinical outcome. Placement patterns also differ in connector lengths and orientations. It tests a mechanical hypothesis, not all biological effects of aging.
