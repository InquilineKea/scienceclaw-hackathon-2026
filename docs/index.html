How much does the same network change when the load turns? Compare stiffness and work to failure across all seven architectures. The slider solves the selected network at any angle; the curves show actual simulations every 15°.

WHERE THE LOAD GOES

Stretch along θ. Let the interior relax.

All 20 perimeter nodes follow u = ε n[n·(X − center)], with n = (cos θ, sin θ). Both coordinates of each perimeter node are prescribed; the 16 interior nodes relax. Cyan squares mark prescribed nodes; green arrows show their motion.

This is constrained directional strain: transverse perimeter strain is zero. It is a different experiment from the free-lateral X/Y grips above. The specimen stays in place as the load rotates. At 180° the strain is identical to 0°.

Stiffness K: conjugate reaction / strain at ε = 0.002. Toughness proxy W: integrated external work until 20% fiber-volume rupture or a reaction drop below half the running peak (after ε > 0.08). The strain cap is 0.85. Unit reference area and thickness make work a density in normalized units.

05 / Keep evidence and assumptions separate

MECHANISMS ≠ CALIBRATION
EXPERIMENTAL CONTEXT

Fibers stiffen, bend, and reorganize.

Primary collagen-network studies support non-affine mechanics and strain-dependent rigidity. They do not validate this generator or its rupture rules.

Licup et al., 2015 ↗Sharma et al., 2016 ↗
OUTSIDE THIS MODEL

Hydration changes the problem.

Hydrostatic pressure, differential pressure and pore pressure are distinct. This network has no solvent, permeability, drainage or shock equation of state. Biaxial compression is a displacement-driven solid-skeleton analogy.

Pressure and hydraulic resistance, 2019 ↗
READ-ONLY MOLECULAR CONTEXT

Fragments cannot calibrate a tissue.

The earlier eight H100 neutral-fragment relaxations and L4 proxy scans are separate evidence. The approximate glucosepane and neutral pyridinoline proxy, model disagreement and boundary minima preclude mapping their curvature values to this model.

Evidence ledger & exclusions ↗

All mechanical outputs are model-derived. No measured inputs are loaded. Nominal volume fraction, diameter, crimp, failure strain, sacrificial bonds, bending and background elasticity are hypothetical controls. There is no crack-area model: work density is not fracture energy.
