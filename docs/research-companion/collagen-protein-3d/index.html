What remains censored?

All 1,082 accepted cases reached 20% strain. Collagen damage appeared in 371; onset was unobserved in 711. None reached the major load-loss criterion (force below 20% of its running maximum after 10% strain). The maximum in this window is not ultimate tensile strength. Only 15 of 511 accepted locked cases showed contact damage: law differences do not isolate irreversibility alone.

Recovery is measured after 20 reduced time units from a 15% preload, with survivor topology frozen during release. A slower recovery is not proof of permanent deformation. Sizes compare finite volumes at fixed density, not mesh refinement.

Earlier commissioning attempts

Version 1 targeted a wider 60% strain window; version 2 stopped on equilibrium-gate failures. The final 20% scope and trust-region fallback were fixed before production. Failed attempts were retained; gates were not relaxed.

Later continuous-fiber follow-up: no-go

Bending and buckling checks passed, but both completed coarse/fine pairs failed the prespecified network refinement gate. Original fitted-modulus differences were 9.71% and 10.46% (limit 5%). A common-strain-sample diagnostic still gave 7.41% and 9.33%; it did not rescue the gate.

Fine repeats: 20 planned, eight started, four completed (two passed per-case checks but failed paired refinement; two timed out), four interrupted and twelve not started. The planned 160-case production comparison did not run. Mesh, loading-step and recovery-timestep changes were combined, so their contributions remain unresolved.
