A slope is not a universal exponent

What this model can tell us

It tests a numerical workflow and shows how strongly an inferred onset depends on its definition. It uses a 16 × 16 precursor lattice with 238–256 retained nodes, periodic boundaries, harmonic stretching and same-fiber angular bending.

This is not a 3D collagen–decorin–fibronectin model. There is no protein binding, assembly, unfolding, excluded volume or thermal motion. Independent affine-start minimizations are not a quasistatic loading path. One size and three seeds do not establish finite-size scaling.
