## Crosslinks and the aging hypothesis

The 6,300-row density experiment comprises seven synthetic network families, three seeds (17,29,43), twelve loading directions (0–165°), one shared zero-added reference and twelve positive connector counts with two placements. Each unchanged 36-node/82-fiber collagen skeleton has fixed collagen volume 0.06. Twenty perimeter nodes undergo prescribed directional strain u=ε n[n·(X−center)], with zero transverse perimeter strain; sixteen interior nodes relax. Initial stiffness is the secant at ε=0.002. Added effective springs use stiffness 0.2 and rupture strain 0.3. Density means count/36 nodes, not chronological age or chemical concentration. Zero means no added model connectors, not collagen without physiological crosslinks.

The density curves use saved 960-step outputs with the original coarse/fine metric-specific qualification flags. Twelve connectors matches the original aging experiment's default parameter settings, not its 480-step numerical outputs. Work to the stopping criterion includes recoverable energy and is not fracture energy or dissipated energy alone. The stopping criterion is 20% collagen-volume rupture or reaction below half its running peak after ε>0.08; the imposed-strain cap is 0.85. Work before first damage is evaluated at the last intact step; missing events remain missing. These endpoints do not identify an experimentally established phase transition.

Ratios are computed against the same seed, angle and network's zero-added reference before averaging. Absolute and paired-ratio views share axes across the two selected networks. Whiskers give the selected-seed minimum and maximum, not confidence intervals. A solid estimate requires all selected seeds to be finite and all original per-metric flags to pass (including the reference for ratios). Finite unqualified estimates are hollow and are not joined by a solid curve. Missing estimates are omitted. The seven-network table's clustered/distributed ratio is paired within seed and omitted unless all selected source flags pass. Placement also changes connector length and orientation; this is not a pure clustering-only intervention.

The force/damage replay is a separate saved example: seed17,24 connectors,0° loading,960 steps. It uses the last stored frame at or below the requested imposed strain, with no interpolation or extrapolation. Beyond a case's available range the last stored state is held and labeled STOPPED. Color/width use one saved absolute force scale across cases. Playback is imposed strain, not physical time. Broken elements never heal in the saved sequence.

## Microrheology and paired estimates

Forty matched specimens span disordered/modular families, distributed/clustered placement, 100/196-node networks and five seeds (17,29,43,67,83). The perimeter is clamped. A virtual four-node probe near (0.35L,0.5L) is driven along x or y at zero prestress. Damage removes the same three original fibers near (0.55L,0.5L); added connectors stay intact. The lesion is near the cluster, so this is not a universal lesion-location test.

The uniform-drag response solves the normalized linear system K/k_ref+iΩI. Ω is not hertz. Local compliance is the driven probe amplitude; remote RMS uses free nodes at distance at least0.6 from the probe. Of960 responses,958 pass the original algebraic residual/finite-positive-response screen. The two damaged static modular/distributed seed43,196-node systems are singular/unresolved; their source zeros are excluded and never interpreted as zero compliance.

The fluid-coupled comparison retains1,600 fine-pressure responses (subdivisionlevel2) from the FEniCSx hybrid discrete-solid/Darcy–Biot study, plus its1,600 per-observable mesh comparisons. Storage0.1, mobilitiesκ=0.001/0.1, drained perimeter pressure and background drag are model assumptions. Solid DOFs are unchanged by pressure refinement. Permeability is unchanged after damage. Static fluid-coupled responses were not computed. Across the original full study,4,158/4,160 systems pass algebraic checks;1,341/1,600 paired observable mesh checks pass. Algebraic resolution does not establish pressure-mesh convergence.

For every displayed frequency, both panels use the same intersection of seeds having valid intact/damaged responses in all selected drive directions. The mesh-qualified-only option also intersects the qualified seeds across both panels. Each damage ratio is formed per direction before averaging directions within seed, then seeds. Directions/frequencies are not independent replicates. Solid points require full selected-seed coverage and, for poroelastic amplitudes/ratios, both intact/damaged amplitude changes≤5% and damage-ratio change≤0.5percentage points on pressure refinement. Partial or mesh-sensitive means are hollow. Radial bins have no corresponding mesh qualification, so poroelastic radial estimates remain hollow even when a selected amplitude passes. Positive frequencies use a logarithmic axis; static results appear only in the selected-frequency summary/radial view where available.

The animated fields are fixed saved examples:196nodes,seed17,xdrive,Ω=0.1; fluid modeκ=0.001, fine pressure mesh. Only the network-family control carries over from ensemble controls. We reconstruct Re[u exp(iφ)]=Re(u)cosφ−Im(u)sinφ, mapping saved free DOF indices to full coordinates. Total and damaged-minus-intact modes each have one fixed arrow scale shared across both patterns and both response models. These are magnified linear phasors, not finite-deformation trajectories; no new damage occurs during a cycle.

## Evidence limits and verification

These crosslink and microrheology datasets are synthetic model experiments. They do not measure human aging, calibrate chemical crosslink mechanics, establish species differences, or supply physical G′/G″/permeability. They remain separate from the measured3D displacement benchmark. No new Gemini/Argon review or paid computation was performed for this extension; it extends the prior Argon-co-designed interface with original native JavaScript/SVG/Canvas code.

Independent validation checks all6,300 crosslink values/flags,960 drag rows,1,600 fine-pressure rows and1,600 mesh records against their owning sources; tests shared zero references, paired exclusions and the original qualified summary; verifies replay dimensions, monotone strain and nonhealing damage; and verifies unchanged measured-field data. Native browser QA checks controls, common-seed exclusions, mobile layout, saved replays and console errors.
