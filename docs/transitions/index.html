What changed after the critique

Strain and connectivity

The shear sweep records tangent stiffness and nonaffine movement. A finite peak is a crossover descriptor, not proof of a critical exponent.

Sliding has its own control

Matched initial spring stiffness is held fixed while a hypothetical locking input changes plastic mobility.

Contact is explicit

Finite disks add contact forces under compression. Their area fraction is not a biological cell volume fraction.

History and stability matter

Residual strain follows load–hold–unload paths. Feedback equilibria need stability checks; loop gain alone does not establish bistability.

Saved states and figures

Animations replay saved protocol states. They are not atomistic trajectories; every frame reports its numerical convergence.

Inspect and reproduce

Raw records retain unconverged points, coordinates, bond states, rest lengths and residual forces. Published comparisons exclude invalid samples. Frozen data are never replaced; later runs receive separate identifiers.
